Medical disclaimer

Last updated 2026-07-28

This website does not diagnose

No page, symptom list or guide on this website can diagnose your condition. Diagnosis is only possible after examination.

Symptom pages

The symptom chips and lists exist only to route you to the right page. Don't use them to conclude what condition you have.

Emergencies

If the eye has been injured, a chemical has entered it, or vision has suddenly gone, don't spend time reading the website. Contact us immediately or go to the nearest hospital.

Medicines and drops

Don't use any medicine or eye drops that weren't prescribed for you. Some drops contain steroids, which can damage the eye with prolonged use.
