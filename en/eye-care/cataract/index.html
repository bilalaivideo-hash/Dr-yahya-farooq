Cataract treatment and surgery in Vehari

A cataract is the eye's natural lens turning cloudy. It is not blindness, and it is not permanent. In a short operation the clouded lens is removed and replaced with a clear artificial one, and sight comes back. The procedure needs no stitches and you go home the same day. What is a cataract?

Inside the eye there is a natural lens that focuses light onto the retina. With age it gradually clouds — like steam settling on clear glass. That is a cataract.

It is not a film growing over the eye, and it does not spread from one eye to the other. Surgery is the only lasting treatment — drops and medicines do not reverse it.

Symptoms

  • A haze over everything
  • Headlights glaring and haloing at night
  • Your prescription changing again and again
  • Colours looking washed out
  • Sight worsening in bright daylight
  • Double vision in one eye

How it is diagnosed

Your sight is tested, drops are used to widen the pupil, and the lens is examined at the slit lamp to see how far the cataract has progressed.

The retina is checked at the same time — because if there is a separate problem at the back of the eye, removing the cataract alone will not fully restore sight. It is important to know that in advance.

The procedure

  1. 01The eye is numbed with drops. You are not put to sleep — you stay awake.00 min
  2. 02A very small opening is made at the edge of the eye. No stitches.00 min
  3. 03The clouded lens is softened and removed.00 min
  4. 04A clear artificial lens is set in its place. It stays for life.00 min
  5. 05The eye is covered and you go home the same day.—

Risks, stated honestly

Every operation carries some risk, and you should know that before you decide. Cataract surgery is the most commonly performed operation in the world and most patients see better afterwards, but in a small number of cases there can be complications such as swelling, infection or raised pressure.

At your examination the doctor will explain what applies to your eye in particular.

Recovery

Day one

The dressing comes off the next day. Some grittiness is normal.

First week

Use the drops on time. Don't touch or rub the eye, keep water out, and stay away from dust.

After that

Vision settles, and a new prescription is made if you need one.

Here, or a bigger city?

It's a fair question and most families ask it. The straight answer: the procedure and the lens are the same ones used in a bigger city. The difference is the journey.

A long drive home with a patched eye is hard on a patient, and every follow-up costs a full day. Here, follow-up is a ten-minute trip, and the doctor who sees you is the one who operated. When you compare cost, count the travel, the attendant's lost day's wages and the time as well.

What to bring

Your current glasses · previous reports and prescriptions · the names of your diabetes and blood-pressure medicines · someone to come with you — don't come alone on the day of surgery.
