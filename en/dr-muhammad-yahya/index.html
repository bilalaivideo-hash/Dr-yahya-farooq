What happens at a consultation

A consultation takes a short time. You'll be asked about the trouble you're having, then your sight is tested, then the eye is examined internally at the slit lamp. Eye pressure is checked where needed.

At the end, the doctor tells you what the problem is, what happens next, and what it will cost.

If drops are used

Some examinations require drops to widen the pupil. Your vision may stay blurred and light-sensitive for a few hours afterwards. Don't drive or ride yourself — bring someone with you.
