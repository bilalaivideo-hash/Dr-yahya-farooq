Before your surgery

  1. Confirm your surgery time by phone one day before.
  2. Bring an adult attendant — do not drive yourself home after surgery.
  3. Bring all previous reports, your diabetes and blood pressure medicines, and your glasses.
  4. Tell your treating doctor about any new redness, discharge or change in symptoms before the planned procedure.
  5. Do not wear makeup, surma or oil on your face.
  6. Eat or fast as instructed by the doctor — ask the staff beforehand.

05 / A few helpful answers

Before you
come in.

Practical answers to help you prepare for your consultation.

Find the clinic ↗
How do I request an appointment?

Choose your doctor and preferred date in the appointment form. On the live website, you can send the request through WhatsApp. Your appointment is confirmed only when the clinic replies.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Walk-in consultations are listed in our OPD schedule. Please confirm the doctor’s availability before travelling. Waiting times depend on the number of patients.

How much does cataract surgery cost?

An estimate is provided after an eye examination and a discussion of the proposed procedure and lens options. Ask the clinic what the estimate includes.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring your glasses, previous eye reports and a list of current medicines. For a planned procedure, follow the instructions provided by your treating doctor.

Do you see patients from outside Vehari?

Patients from Burewala, Mailsi, Luddan, Karampur and nearby areas can plan a visit to our G Block clinic. Please confirm the schedule before travelling.

Where is Ismail Eye & General Clinic?

The clinic address supplied is 167/1, G Block, Vehari 61100, Punjab, Pakistan. Use the directions link to search for the clinic on Google Maps.
